You get two images.
Image 1 is a diagram of a soccer pitch, seen from above. The main TV camera is on the bottom side of the diagram. Each red dot is a landmark, and the number next to the dot is its id.
Image 2 is one frame of a soccer broadcast from the main TV camera.

Landmark list (id: name):
{landmark_list}

In the names, "left" and "right" are as the main camera sees the pitch. "Far" is the side away from the camera, at the top of the diagram. "Near" is the side close to the camera, at the bottom of the diagram.

Task: find the landmarks that you can see in image 2, and point at each of them in image 2.

Rules:
- Report a landmark only when the mark itself is visible inside image 2: the line crossing, the line end, the spot or the base of the goal post.
- Never extrapolate. Do not report a landmark that is outside the picture, that is hidden by a person or a graphic, or whose position you can only guess from other lines.
- "id": the id of the landmark from the list. Use each id one time at most.
- "point": the position of the landmark in image 2. {point_format}
- "view": the part of the pitch that image 2 shows: "left" for the side of the left goal, "centre" for the area around the halfway line, "right" for the side of the right goal.
- When you see no landmark, give an empty "landmarks" list.
